# The First Day

Martha opened the window and let the cool morning air into the room. Outside, the city was only just waking up.

She thought that **today everything might change**. She didn't know yet how right she was.

## An hour later

She went down the stairs, counting the steps the way she had since she was a child. *Maybe it's a silly habit* — she thought — *but it works*.

Thomas was already waiting downstairs, leaning against the doorframe.

— You slept in — he said.

— Not as long as you did yesterday — she replied, smiling faintly.

✦

That evening, as the sun sank behind the neighboring rooftops, Martha sat by the window with a cup of tea. The day that had begun so ordinarily was ending with something she couldn't yet name.
